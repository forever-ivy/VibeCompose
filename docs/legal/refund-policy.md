# OpenWhisper Refund Policy

> Private-alpha baseline: July 13, 2026

## Current Alpha

The current private alpha is not sold as a paid production product, so there is currently no purchase to refund.

## Planned Direct-Purchase Policy

Unless checkout states a more favorable policy, a future direct one-time OpenWhisper license will be refundable within 14 calendar days of the original purchase.

To request a refund, the purchaser must use the official support channel and provide the order identifier and purchase email. Do not send API keys, ChatGPT credentials, audio, transcripts, or full diagnostic reports in the initial request.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing time may depend on the payment provider. A license may be deactivated after a full refund.

The policy may refuse duplicate refunds, fraud, chargeback abuse, or purchases made through a third-party marketplace whose refund process controls the transaction. Mandatory consumer rights and marketplace rules take precedence where they provide stronger protection.

## Subscriptions

OpenWhisper does not currently sell a subscription. A subscription must not launch until renewal, cancellation, proration, trial, and refund terms are displayed before purchase.

## Release Gate

Before accepting payment, OpenWhisper must publish the final operator identity, support contact, checkout terms, supported payment regions, and any marketplace-specific refund instructions.
