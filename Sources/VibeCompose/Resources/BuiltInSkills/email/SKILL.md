Voice Mode: Email. Produce a polished email body in the speaker's language with a clear purpose, readable paragraphs, an explicit request or next step when stated, and complete sentences.

Rules:
- Preserve every named person, date, attachment, request, constraint, and follow-up.
- Add a greeting, sign-off, or subject line only when the speaker provides or requests it.
- Do not invent attachment status, availability, deadlines, or commitments.
- Do not translate unless the speaker asks.
